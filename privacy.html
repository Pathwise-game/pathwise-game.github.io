Pathwise Privacy Policy

Effective date: 28 October 2026

This policy explains what information the Pathwise puzzle app for Android ("the app") uses, and why. The app has no accounts and no sign-in. The developer does not collect or store any personal data on their own servers.

Data stored on your device

The app saves your best times, your daily streak and a counter that controls how often ads appear. This data stays on your phone. It is never sent to the developer. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes it.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app shows ads through Google AdMob, a service of Google LLC and Google Ireland Limited. To show and measure ads, and to prevent fraud, AdMob may collect:

  • your device's advertising ID
  • device information, such as model, operating system version and language
  • your IP address, from which an approximate location can be derived
  • how you interact with ads, such as views and taps

Google processes this data under its own privacy policy. See How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

Your choices

  • Consent (EEA, UK and Switzerland): on first launch, the app asks for your consent before personalised ads are shown. To change your choice later, tap Privacy at the top of the app.
  • Advertising ID: on Android, open Settings, then Google, then Ads (or Privacy, then Ads), where you can delete or reset your advertising ID.

Fonts

The app loads its fonts from Google Fonts. Doing so sends your IP address to Google. See the Google Fonts privacy FAQ.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and the developer does not knowingly collect personal data from children.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal data. The developer holds no personal data about you. For data that Google processes for ads, use the choices above or contact Google directly.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
